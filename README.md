# DataOlllo

**Open, clean, and analyze CSV files on your computer.**

DataOlllo is a desktop app for preparing and analyzing local data without writing code. Use it to explore large CSV files, clean exports, and turn repeat tasks into reusable workflows.

[Download DataOlllo](https://www.dataolllo.com/download) · [Getting started](https://www.dataolllo.com/docs/getting-started) · [Documentation](https://www.dataolllo.com/docs/introduction) · [Contact](https://www.dataolllo.com/contact)

## What you can do

- **Inspect and filter:** Open a CSV, explore its columns, and select the rows you need.
- **Clean and combine:** Remove duplicates, transform columns, and merge or split files.
- **Summarize and visualize:** Group records, calculate summaries, and create charts.
- **Repeat your work:** Save steps as a workflow and apply them to a similar file.
- **Ask for help:** Use the optional AI assistant to help explore data and build workflows.

DataOlllo is available for Windows and macOS. Capacity and performance depend on your computer, your data, and the operation.

## Try a first workflow

1. [Download the app](https://www.dataolllo.com/download) and open a CSV file.
2. Filter or clean the data, then group and summarize the result.
3. Save your steps and apply the workflow to another file with a similar structure.

For a guided example, see [Your First Workflow](https://www.dataolllo.com/docs/your-first-workflow). You can also try the [free browser CSV tools](https://www.dataolllo.com/csv-tools).

## Privacy and AI

Core file processing runs locally on your computer. Optional AI features can use local models or online providers, depending on your configuration. Online providers may receive prompts and the context you supply; review your settings and the [privacy policy](https://www.dataolllo.com/privacy-policy) before working with sensitive data.
